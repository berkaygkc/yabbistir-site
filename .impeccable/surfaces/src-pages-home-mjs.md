# Surface: yabbistir.com (home, extension store, extension pages, privacy)

Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: Mac users and developers arriving from HN, Reddit, YouTube, AlternativeTo and LinkedIn. Job: understand in seconds, try the panel, trust it, download. Second job: browse the 36 store extensions and install one through `yabbistir://store/install?id=…` (app 0.5.0+), with a download fallback. Proof: two films, the live demo panel, the signed catalog's real data, Developer ID + notarization, MIT, no telemetry. No star or download counts.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Paste was a physical craft before ⌘V. The site is a paste-up board where every fact is a galley pressed into place, and the headline itself lands 8 pt under a caret the way the panel does. It refuses the dark Mac-utility hero with a glowing screenshot over a feature-card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Illustration-board white under a non-photo-blue modular grid with pica ruler ticks; white galley strips with soft offset shadows and real slug lines (version, id, permissions); black hairline crop marks and registration targets; rubylith red film reserved for the one primary action and text selection; blue-pencil measurement notes. Franklin Gothic headlines, typeset serif body, typewriter slugs. Dark mode is a backlit light table.

STORY: The visitor sees the mechanism enacted, types into the live panel and pastes into the draft, learns it grows through extensions that state what they may do, trusts the facts, downloads.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Slug-line nav on top. Left seven columns: a typed draft line ending in a blinking caret; 8 pt below it the H1 galley "Paste where you type." with a blue 8 pt measure; the subhead; a rubylith Download tab with the brew slug beside it. Right five columns: the live panel as a pasted photo with crop marks and a blue note "type · ↑ ↓ · ↵".

FORM: Montaj Masası (paste-up board), position 1 on the ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, chosen by the user), seed key 4c6082e9. Build path: code-led. Signature interaction: the live panel under the draft caret; ↵ pastes the chosen row into the draft. Motion grammar: galleys settle onto the board (lift, slide, press, shadow tightens) once, when they enter.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
